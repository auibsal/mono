The Society's identity: the symbol, the two wordmarks and the lockups built from them. All of them are vector, with text outlined (Ubuntu Bold and Ubuntu Arabic Bold), so no fonts are needed. Use them as `<img>`; their colours are fixed, so never recolour them.

**Colour (one hue).** Files without a suffix are for light grounds (`white`, `paper`, `crimson-50`): crimson `#9c213e` symbol, Crimson 700 `#7e1a32` wordmarks. `-reversed` files are for `crimson` and `ink` grounds: white symbol, Crimson 100 `#ebcbd3` wordmarks. They are invisible on white.

**Lockups**
- `sal-lockup-horizontal(-reversed).svg`: symbol + English wordmark. The default.
- `sal-lockup-bilingual(-reversed).svg`: English | symbol | Arabic. For anything public.
- `sal-lockup-stacked(-reversed).svg`: symbol over the English wordmark. For square spaces, stickers and merchandise.

**Parts**
- `sal-symbol.svg` (crimson) and `sal-symbol-white.svg`: the symbol alone, for favicons, stamps and badges.
- `sal-wordmark-en(-reversed).svg`: "AUIB / Society of / Arts and / Letters".
- `sal-wordmark-ar(-reversed).svg`: «جمعيةُ الفنونِ / والآدابِ في / الجامعةِ / الأمريكيةِ».
- `sal-avatar.svg`: white symbol on a crimson square, for profile pictures.

**Proportions.** In lockups the symbol is as tall as the English wordmark, from the first cap line to the last baseline. The gap between symbol and text equals the depth of the notch. Clear space is one notch depth on every side.

**Minimum sizes.** Symbol: 24 px tall on screen or 6 mm printed. Horizontal lockup: 45 mm wide in print. Below that, use the symbol alone.

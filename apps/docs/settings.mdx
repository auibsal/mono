---
title: Settings
description: Semesters, contact details, the Journal's name and features.
---

**Administration → Settings** holds what the Society changes without a
developer:

- **Semesters and blackout dates.**
- **Contact and the Faculty Advisor:** the Society email, the link to The
  Common Room on Telegram and the Faculty Advisor's name. They appear on
  auibsal.org/contact and the Nexus Society page.
- **The Journal's name** in both languages.
- **Pledge versions:** raising one asks every member to accept it again.
- **Feature flags:** switch elections on or off.

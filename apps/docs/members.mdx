title: Members, roles and elections
description: Verification, roles and the elections switch.
---

## Verification

AUIB addresses are verified when they confirm their email. Other addresses
(alumni, guests) wait in **Administration → Members** for an officer to
approve or decline.

## Roles

Assign and end roles in **Administration → Members**. A role grants
permissions, sometimes only for one program, issue or campaign. Council
roles need two-step sign-in before they work.

## Elections

Elections are switched off until the Elections Committee is ready. When the
switch is on (**Settings → Feature flags**), members see nominations, the ranked
ballot and results on the Nexus Society page.

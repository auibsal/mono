## The pipeline

1. **Call.** An editor opens a call for an issue, with its dates and the
   limit per person.
2. **Intake.** The Submissions Manager checks formatting and returns work
   that needs fixing. They hold the key between blind ids and names, and
   never score.
3. **Blind reading.** Readers score each piece from its blind id. When they
   disagree, a third reader decides.
4. **Selection.** The editors choose the issue from the scored work.
5. **Decision.** Authors hear by email either way. Accepted work is
   published only after the author signs the Publication Agreement.

Readers and editors never see an author's name before a decision.

## Publishing

**Administration → Journal: issues and pieces.** Publish pieces and issues
when they are ready, or schedule them.

## News

**Administration → Content → News → New post.** Write the title in both
languages and the text, then **Publish**. Two editors can work on the same
post at once; each sees the other's changes as they type.

## Pages

Pages are for longer standing text. Two are waiting to be written:

- **The Founders' Roll**: create a page with the address `about/founders`.
- **Traditions** (Charter Night, the Ribbon, the Term Card): create a page
  with the address `about/traditions`.

## Notices

Notices appear on members' Nexus home pages, or as a banner on auibsal.org,
for the dates you choose.

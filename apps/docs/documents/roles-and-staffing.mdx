## How SAL is organised

Four layers, each with a clear job. Members hold the power, the Council runs the work, departments carry it out, and programmes are where the art happens.

1. **The General Assembly.** Every member. Meets at least once a semester. Elects the four officers, changes the Constitution, receives the accounts and can remove anyone who abuses a role.
2. **The Council.** Four elected officers (the Executive Board) and six appointed directors. Meets every two weeks, adopts the budget, greenlights programmes, makes the Bylaws and Policies.
3. **Six departments.** Letters · Stage & Arts · Programmes & Events · Media · Partnerships & Outreach · Membership & Community. Each director leads a small team of coordinators.
4. **Programmes.** The things people see: Waraq, Side Quest, Second Chapter, the Typewriter Tour, the Majlis and more. Each has a lead, a charter and a budget, and the big ones have their own handbook.

## Growing in stages

We staff for the Society we are, not the one we hope to be. Each stage adds roles only when the work and the people are there.

| Stage | Members | Council | Other roles | People in roles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Founding, 2026–27 | Up to ~40 registered | 8 seats: four officers + Letters (also Stage & Arts), Programmes, Media, Membership. The VP covers Partnerships. | 4 programme chairs; 6–8 coordinators (Social Media, Design, Onboarding, Event Coordinator, Archivist, Majlis Host); programme teams | ~18 + programme teams |
| 2 · Growing, 2027–28 | 40–100 | All 10 seats filled | ~14 coordinators; Scratch Night and a one-act festival; Alumni Lead | ~28 |
| 3 · Established, 2028 onward | 100+ | 10 seats + deputies for the Treasurer and General Secretary | ~20 coordinators; Sponsorship Lead; inter-university links | ~35 |

- **Move up a stage when…** Voting Members pass the next band for a full semester, and every existing seat has been filled for that semester.
- **Hold back when…** Seats sit empty, deadlines slip, or the same five people do everything. Fill and train before adding.
- **Who decides.** The Council, at the start of each semester, recorded in the minutes. Stages are a guide, not a rule.

<Tip>
  **The smallest working Society.** If people are scarce, these six must be filled first: President, Vice President, Treasurer, General Secretary, Media Director and Director of Programmes. Everything else can be covered for a semester.
</Tip>

## Staffing principles

1. **Two roles at most, one leadership role.** Nobody holds more than two Society roles, and only one of them may be officer, director or programme chair (B5.5). The founding term is the only exception.
2. **Every role has a backup.** Each role card names a deputy. If you are ill during an event week, the work still happens.
3. **Honest hours.** Each role says how many hours it needs. Applicants tell us their other commitments. We would rather fill a role late than burn someone out.
4. **Open doors, fair choices.** Every role is advertised in the Open Call to every student, SAL member or not, and chosen by a panel with a score sheet.
5. **Try before you commit.** Team roles start with a three-week Apprenticeship: one real task with a mentor, then a free choice on both sides.
6. **Write it down, hand it on.** Every role keeps a handover dossier from its first week, not its last.
7. **Credit is part of the job.** Names in programmes and mastheads, certificates of service, and Fellowships for those who give the most.

## The Executive Board

The four elected officers. Together they prepare every Council meeting and carry the Society between them.

### President

Elected · Reports to the General Assembly · Backup: Vice President · Time: 8–10 h/week · Term: one year; two terms max

Leads the Society, keeps it true to its mission, and makes sure the right people have what they need to do their work.

**Responsibilities**
- Chairs the Council and the General Assembly; sets the agenda with the General Secretary
- Represents SAL to the Office of Student Life, faculty and partners; signs letters and agreements
- Holds each officer and director to their work plan through a monthly one-to-one
- Serves ex officio as Publisher of Waraq: approvals, budget, editorial independence
- Approves spending up to 250,000 IQD with the Treasurer
- Keeps the Society Typewriter; opens Charter Night and the AGM
- Writes the Annual Report and leads the handover to the next President

**Decides:** Council agenda and meeting chair rulings · Who speaks for SAL in public · Emergency suspension (with the VP) where safety is at risk

**Doing well looks like:** Every Council seat filled or covered · Every programme has a charter and a lead · Annual Report delivered at the AGM · A successor ready: at least two credible candidates

**Hands on:** Student Life contacts and open requests · Partner agreements and their renewal dates · The Society Typewriter and its ribbon

Founding term: Shaheen Farjo (Founder; founding term)

### Vice President

Elected · Reports to the President · Backup: General Secretary · Time: 5–6 h/week · Term: one year

The Society's second engine: runs the directors day to day, keeps projects on schedule, and chairs the Conduct Panel so that fairness has an owner.

**Responsibilities**
- Acts for the President when absent and chairs items the President speaks on
- Collects each director's semester work plan and checks progress every two weeks
- Chairs the Conduct Panel; keeps concerns confidential and on time
- Leads the Open Call and the appointment panels with the President
- Owns the semester calendar and resolves clashes between programmes
- Covers Partnerships & Outreach while that seat is vacant in the founding term

**Decides:** Programme calendar clashes · Conduct Panel procedure (with the Panel)

**Doing well looks like:** Directors' work plans in by week 3 · 90% of dated actions done on time · Conduct cases closed within 14 days of hearing

**Hands on:** Calendar, work plans and progress log · Open conduct matters (restricted)

Founding term: to be appointed by Charter Day

### General Secretary

Elected · Reports to the President · Backup: Vice President · Time: 4–5 h/week · Term: one year

The memory of the Society: minutes, records, the register, the archive, and every letter to the University.

**Responsibilities**
- Sends agendas 48 hours before and minutes within 72 hours; keeps the Decisions Log
- Single point of contact for the Office of Student Life: room bookings, approvals, all-student emails
- Keeps the Constitution, Bylaws and Policies current, with version records
- Runs election administration unless standing; supports the Elections Committee
- Keeps the archive and the Book of Members with the Archivist
- Holds the Society Drive structure and access permissions with the Media Director

**Decides:** Drive structure and file naming · What is archived permanently

**Doing well looks like:** Minutes out within 72 hours, every time · Every Student Life request filed on the lead times in the Playbook · Archive complete for each semester

**Hands on:** Drive admin rights · Register and archive · Student Life request log

Founding term: to be appointed by Charter Day

### Treasurer

Elected · Reports to the President and Council · Backup: President (Deputy Treasurer from Stage 3) · Time: 3–4 h/week; more in event weeks · Term: one year

Keeps every dinar honest and visible, so that the University, partners and members can trust the Society with money.

**Responsibilities**
- Drafts the semester budget from directors' requests; brings it to Council by week 4
- Keeps the ledger in the Operations Tracker; files every receipt within 48 hours
- Co-approves spending up to 50,000 IQD with directors, and up to 250,000 IQD with the President
- Trains cashiers and enforces the two-person cash rule at every event
- Pays reimbursements within 14 days of a complete claim
- Reports to Council every other meeting and to the AGM each year; prepares the Audit

**Decides:** Whether a claim is complete · Cash procedure at events

**Doing well looks like:** Ledger matches receipts at the Audit · Spending within 10% of budget · Charity handovers witnessed and receipted

**Hands on:** Ledger and receipts folder · Cash box, float log and keys · Open claims and pledges

Founding term: to be appointed by Charter Day

## The Directors

Six appointed directors, each leading a department and a small team of coordinators.

### Director of Letters

Appointed · Reports to the Vice President · Backup: a coordinator named each semester · Time: 4–6 h/week · Term: one year

Keeps reading and writing at the heart of the Society: the salon, the workshops, the book circle and the prizes.

**Responsibilities**
- Runs the Majlis (monthly salon), Open Pages (writing workshops) and the Book Circle, through their hosts
- Liaises with Waraq's Editor-in-Chief; the journal stays editorially independent
- Designs the SAL Prizes for writing in Arabic and English (from Year 2)
- Invites guest writers with the Director of Partnerships
- Keeps the Society's reading list and book-swap shelf
- Covers Stage & Arts while that seat is vacant in the founding term

**Decides:** Salon and workshop themes and guests (within budget) · Book Circle titles, alternating Arabic and English

**Doing well looks like:** One Majlis a month in term · Two Open Pages workshops a semester · Average attendance rising semester on semester

**Hands on:** Guest writer contacts · Reading list and shelf inventory

Founding term: open · SAL Open Call, Oct 18

### Director of Stage & Arts

Appointed · Reports to the Vice President · Backup: Stage Manager · Time: 4–6 h/week; more in production weeks · Term: one year

Builds SAL's theatre and visual arts, step by step, from a first staged reading to a full production.

**Responsibilities**
- Runs Scratch Night: an informal showcase where anyone tries a scene, poem or sketch
- Leads the theatre ladder: staged readings, then a one-act festival, then a full production
- Curates the Gallery Wall of student art and photography
- Works with the Director of Programmes on venues, rehearsal rooms and technical needs
- Keeps rights clear: permission for any published play, credit for every artist

**Decides:** Casting (with each director of a piece) · Gallery selection

**Doing well looks like:** One Scratch Night a semester · First one-act festival in Year 2 · Every performance has a stage manager and a risk check

**Hands on:** Scripts and rights file · Props and costume inventory

Founding term: covered by the Director of Letters

### Director of Programmes & Events

Appointed · Reports to the Vice President · Backup: Event Coordinator · Time: 5–6 h/week · Term: one year

Turns ideas into events that happen on time, safely, in the right room, with the right approvals.

**Responsibilities**
- Keeps the events calendar and the Term Card; checks every event against exams and other clubs
- Runs the event process in the Playbook: proposal, approvals, rooms, run sheet, debrief
- Books rooms and equipment through the General Secretary and Student Life
- Leads the volunteer rota and briefings with the Director of Membership
- Owns the risk check (Form F-11) for every event over 30 people
- Runs Charter Night each October and the AGM logistics each April

**Decides:** Run sheets and on-the-day decisions (safety first) · Whether an event is ready to go ahead at the T–7 check

**Doing well looks like:** Every event filed on the Playbook lead times · A debrief within three days of each event · No event cancelled for missing approvals

**Hands on:** Venue contacts and booking log · Event kit inventory

Founding term: open · SAL Open Call, Oct 18

### Media Director

Appointed · Reports to the Vice President · Backup: Social Media Lead · Time: 5–6 h/week · Term: one year

Owns how SAL looks and sounds everywhere: Instagram, Telegram, video, design and photography.

**Responsibilities**
- Runs @auibsal and The Common Room with the Social Media Lead; keeps a two-week content calendar
- Guards the brand: the design system, templates and sub-brands
- Leads photo and video at every event, with consent and the camera-shy rules
- Serves as Creative Director of Side Quest in the founding term
- Approves routine posts; escalates sensitive content to the President
- Keeps the media archive backed up in two places

**Decides:** Routine posts and the content calendar · Design of Society materials within the brand guide

**Doing well looks like:** Posting at least three times a week in term · Every event photographed and archived within a week · Follower growth and saves tracked monthly

**Hands on:** Account admin rights (two admins minimum) · Brand files and templates · Media archive locations

Founding term: Ali Hayder

### Director of Partnerships & Outreach

Appointed · Reports to the President · Backup: Club Relations Lead · Time: 3–5 h/week · Term: one year

Connects SAL to other clubs, departments, charities and cultural institutions, and makes sure every partnership is written down and kept.

**Responsibilities**

### Director of Membership & Community

Appointed · Reports to the Vice President · Backup: Onboarding Lead · Time: 3–5 h/week · Term: one year

Makes SAL easy to join and hard to leave: the register, the welcome, the recognition and the traditions.

**Responsibilities**
- Keeps the register and the attendance record from which voting rights are calculated
- Runs the Welcome: new-member evenings, the six words on the typewriter, the first-month buddy
- Runs the Apprenticeship for team roles with each director

**Decides:** Welcome programme and member communications (with Media) · Who is in good standing, from the record

## Advisors

Two roles that sit beside the Council without a vote.

### Faculty Advisor

Invited · Reports to the Office of Student Life · Time: about 2 h/month · Term: one year, renewable

A trusted adult in the room: advice, signatures and a steady hand on sensitive matters, without directing the art.

**Responsibilities**
- Joins one Council meeting a month and the General Assemblies
- Signs the approvals the University requires
- Reviews the accounts each semester, or names the Audit
- First contact for sensitive issues; hears appeals under B10.6 and B6.10
- Signs certificates of service

**Decides:** Appeals on conduct and elections (final)

**Doing well looks like:** Approvals signed on time · Audit complete each semester

**Hands on:** Briefing for the next Advisor

Founding term: to be confirmed by Oct 8

### Founder

Honorary, permanent · Time: as agreed · Term: for life

The person who started the Society. Serves as President through the founding term, then as Founding Advisor for one year, then as a friend of the Society.

**Responsibilities**
- Founding term: President, with all the duties above
- Appoints the founding officers and directors for confirmation
- After office: Founding Advisor to the Council for one year, without a vote
- Opens Charter Night each year if able

**Decides:** Nothing beyond any office held

**Doing well looks like:** A Society that runs well without its Founder

**Hands on:** The founding story, written down for the archive

Founding term: Shaheen Farjo

**What every chair does**
- Keeps a one-page Programme Charter (Form F-29) current
- Recruits their team through the Open Call
- Submits the programme budget to the Treasurer by week 2
- Reports within three weeks of each edition (Form F-25 B)

- Editorial and creative independence within the charter (Constitution 10.3)
- Room bookings and approvals through the General Secretary
- A budget decided before the work starts

- Written reasons for any decision that overrules them

## Succession and backup

Student societies usually die in the year after their founders graduate. Ours is designed to survive that year.

| If this person is away… | …this person steps in | …and is being prepared to succeed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| President | Vice President | Vice President or a director with a full year |
| Vice President | General Secretary | Any director |
| General Secretary | Vice President | Archivist |
| Treasurer | President (second signature: VP) | Deputy Treasurer or a trained cashier |
| Any director | Their named coordinator | That coordinator |
| Programme chair | Their deputy in the programme | Deputy or section lead |

**The founder's year.** The first election is in April 2027. From then, the Founder holds office only if elected, and may sit with the Council for one year as Founding Advisor, without a vote. By Charter Night 2027, the Society should be run entirely by people who joined after it was founded.

**Shadow, then hand over.** Newly elected officers shadow their predecessors from the AGM (April 20) to handover (May 13). Every role keeps a handover dossier (F-24), and access to every account moves within seven days.

## Pathways

Nobody needs permission to get more involved. This is the usual path, but any member may apply for any role.

1. **Friend:** follows @auibsal, comes to a Majlis
2. **Member:** registers, signs the Pledge, types six words
3. **Voting Member:** two recorded activities in a semester
4. **Apprentice:** three weeks, one real task, a mentor
5. **Coordinator:** owns a piece of a department
6. **Director or Chair:** leads a department or a programme
7. **Officer:** elected by the members
8. **Fellow:** honoured for service; for life

**What members take away.** A named role on a real publication, production or event; a certificate of service signed by the Faculty Advisor; a reference from the President on request; and, for those who give the most, a Fellowship for life.

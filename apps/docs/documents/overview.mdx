## Which document wins

Constitution 15.2: where documents conflict, this order prevails:

1. University policy
2. The Constitution
3. The Bylaws
4. The Policies
5. Handbooks and guides (including this site)

## How a document changes

| Document | Who changes it | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitution | General Assembly | Two-thirds of votes cast, 14 days' notice (16.1–16.3); sections 1.1, 2.4, 3.1 and 3.4 need two Assemblies 30 days apart (16.4) |
| Bylaws and Policies | Council | Two-thirds of Council, published within seven days; members may overturn at the next General Assembly by simple majority (15.1, B12.3) |
| Handbooks and guides | The owner, with the Council | Reviewed each year |

When a document is adopted or amended, the General Secretary updates its entry in the registry. That changes the status shown here, on auibsal.org and in the Nexus at the same time.

## Documents referred to but not published

The published documents cite working documents that role holders keep in SAL Drive: the Operations Playbook, the Templates & Forms Pack (SAL-OPS-02, the F- forms and L- letters), the Operations Tracker, and each programme's own package (the Journal's Masthead Handbook, the Side Quest Production Bible, the Second Chapter Operations Kit). Ask the General Secretary for access.

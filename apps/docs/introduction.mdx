title: The Nexus for officers
description: What the Nexus is, who sees what, and where to start.
---

The Nexus (nexus.auibsal.org) is where members book events, send work to the
AUIB Literary Journal and sign up for shifts, and where officers run the
Society: events, the Journal, news, the charity ledger, members and settings.

## Who sees what

Every screen in **Administration** depends on your role. The database itself
checks your role on every read and write, so a section you can't open is one
your role doesn't cover, not a fault. Ask the President or the General
Secretary if you need access.

- **Council officers, directors and the Elections Committee** sign in with a
  second step (a code from an authenticator app). See [Signing in](/sign-in).
- **Program leads, editors and event staff** see only their own program,
  issue or event.

## Where things appear

| You change | It appears |
| --- | --- |
| A published event | auibsal.org/events and the Nexus Events page |
| A published news post | auibsal.org/news, within a minute |
| A Journal piece or issue | auibsal.org/journal |
| A signed-off ledger entry | The Warmth Meter on auibsal.org/give |
| Contact details in Settings | auibsal.org/contact |

Drafts never appear publicly.
